Key takeaways Growth equity remains an attractive strategy, but the opportunity set is becoming more selective. AI is shortening product cycles and compressing the period over which product-market fit stays differentiated, placing greater weight on asset quality and a manager’s ability to assess durability. Downside protection remains structural, while AI expands the upside.
