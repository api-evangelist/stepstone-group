(Nasdaq: STEP) today announced that Mike McCabe, Head of Strategy, and Jason Ment, President and Co-Chief Operating Officer, are scheduled to present at the Barclays Global Financial Services Conference on Monday, September 14, 2026, at 9:00 am ET. A live webcast and replay will be accessible through the StepStone website at https://shareholders.stepstonegroup.com.
